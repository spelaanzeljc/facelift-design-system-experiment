Shadows & Spacing

Five shadow tokens for elevation, and eleven spacing steps for consistent layout rhythm. Always use token variables — never hardcode box-shadow values or pixel measurements.

Shadows

5 tokens

Shadow tokens are split by context: shadows-shadow-md is a general-purpose elevation, card-widget-* targets widget cards, and media-item-* targets thumbnail/media surfaces.
